When restyling the Erasmus+ MMG application, preserve its original color palette and keep the navigation sidebar expandable/collapsible.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to retain the original colors and clarified that the sidebar must be expandable.

**How to apply:** Treat layout and spacing as flexible; retain the MMG navy, blue, teal, and light surfaces, and provide an accessible sidebar toggle with labels when expanded.
